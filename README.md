# R.E.P.O. Mod Menu

A free in-game menu for **R.E.P.O.** on Windows. See what's around you, tweak your player, deal with enemies, and use host tools in your own lobby.

The current version is **v1.0.3**. It has **54 features in 10 groups**. It works by loading the menu into the running game.

## What you can do

- **Player:** god mode, infinite stamina, movement boost, unlimited jumps, more health and stamina, long reach and stronger grabs.
- **Enemies:** make them ignore you, freeze or blind them, kill them all, or lure and gather them where you look or where you throw.
- **Valuables:** unbreakable valuables, infinite battery, a lower haul goal, and sending valuables straight to extraction.
- **ESP:** enemies, valuables, items and players, plus markers for the truck and the extraction points. A gun aim line, grab target, throw arc and haul counter are on screen too.
- **Spawning:** spawn items and enemies, delete what you look at, revive or kill players, and revive everyone.
- **Levels and saves:** skip ahead, go to the shop, pick the level number, keep the save when everyone dies, and back up or restore your saves.
- **Extras:** cash and upgrades, a developer console and all cosmetics unlocked.

The full feature list, changelog and FAQ are on the project page: **https://sollerto.de/cheats/stcheat-repo**

## How to use it

1. Download the file from the project page and run it.
2. Start the game. It doesn't matter which one you launch first.
3. Press `Insert` in the game to open and close the menu.

## Before you run it

- **Back up your saves first.**
- **Antivirus programs often flag tools like this**, because they read and change another program's memory. Compare the file's SHA-256 with the one on the project page. A match only means you have the file the site published.
- **Use it at your own risk.** Many games ban players who cheat.
