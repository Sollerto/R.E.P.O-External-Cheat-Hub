# STCheat for R.E.P.O.: features

> Summarized by Claude Opus 5.5 - Information might not be 100% accurate

**Dowloads at [sollerto.de](https://sollerto.de)**

Press **Insert** in the game to open the menu. Anything marked **HOST** only works when you host the game (or play solo); everything else also works in a friend's game. Any switch or button can get its own hotkey: right-click it and press a key.

**Free and members:** without an account you get God mode, Infinite stamina, Movement boost and Unlimited jumps, the whole ESP and Teleport tabs, and the menu settings. Everything else is marked **MEMBER** in the menu and unlocks when you sign in to [sollerto.de](https://sollerto.de) (Menu tab, Sign in). The Offline download has no sign-in and everything unlocked (UnknownCheats upload).

## Player

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| God mode | Nothing hurts you. |
| Infinite stamina | Sprint and jump as much as you like. |
| Movement boost | Faster, higher, floatier. Speed, jump height, gravity and extra air jumps are adjustable. |
| Unlimited jumps | Jump again in the air as often as you like. |
| Max health x | More max health without upgrades. The other players see it too. |
| Max stamina x | More max stamina without upgrades. |
| Long reach | Grab things from far away (up to 150 m), and hold them farther out. |
| Grab strength x **HOST** | Lift, drag and throw heavy things easily. Strength and throw power are adjustable. |
| No overcharge | Hold enemies as long as you like without your beam overcharging and zapping you. |
| Enemies don't break free **HOST** | Grabbed enemies stay held. Optionally any enemy can be grabbed, whatever your strength. |

The multipliers keep working through new levels and upgrades you buy.

## Cash & upgrades (HOST)

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Cash | Set the run's money. |
| Your upgrades | Set each of your upgrade levels: health, stamina, sprint speed, extra jump, grab strength, grab range, throw strength, tumble launch, tumble wings, tumble climb, crouch rest, map player count, death head battery. |

## Enemies

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Damage x **HOST** | Everything you and your friends do to enemies hurts more. |
| Enemies ignore you **HOST** | Enemies don't see you. |
| Kill all enemies **HOST** | Every enemy in the level dies. |
| Freeze enemies **HOST** | Every enemy stops where it is. |
| Blind enemies **HOST** | Enemies can't see anyone, but they still hear noises and come to look. |
| Gather enemies where you look **HOST** | Every enemy jumps to the spot under your crosshair. |
| Lure enemies to where you look **HOST** | Enemies nearby come to check out the spot, like after a loud noise. The radius is adjustable. |
| Lure enemies to what you throw **HOST** | When something you threw lands (a trap, a grenade...), nearby enemies come to look at it. |

## Valuables & items

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Unbreakable valuables **HOST** | Valuables don't break or lose value. |
| Infinite battery **HOST** | Every item's battery stays full. |
| Low haul goal **HOST** | From the next level, the goal is $1K per extraction point. |
| Send valuables to extraction | Puts the valuables closest to the active extraction point inside it, one by one. Choose how many, and whether to stop at its goal. |

## Aim & HUD

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Gun aim line | While you hold a gun: where its shot goes. Red when it would hit an enemy. |
| Grab target | What your grab beam would catch and how far away it is. Green when it's in reach. |
| Throw arc | While you hold something: where it lands if you let go now. |
| Haul counter | Extracted so far and the level's goal, the current extraction point's progress, extraction points done, and how much loot is still on the map. Pick the corner. |

## ESP

Shows things through walls, only on your screen.

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Enemies | Name and distance, with boxes, health bars (**HOST**), lines and arrows at the screen edge for ones behind you. |
| Valuables | Value, name and distance. Can hide anything below a minimum value. |
| Items | Weapons, tools, drones and upgrades lying around. |
| Players | Name, health and distance, with off-screen arrows. Dead players' heads too. |

Each kind has its own range, boxes and lines. Text size, distances and the most shown per kind are adjustable.

## Teleport

Moves only you.

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Truck | Into the truck. |
| Extraction point | The active one, or else the closest one that isn't done. |
| Other extraction point | Any of them, from a list with their state and distance. |
| Player | Right next to someone, or to their head if they're dead. |
| Where you're looking | The spot under your crosshair, up to 300 m away. |
| Back | Where you were before the last teleport. |

## Host tools (HOST)

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Spawn item | Pick any item from a searchable list and spawn it where you look. Bind a key to spawn more. |
| Spawn enemy | Pick any enemy type and spawn it near where you look. |
| Delete what you're looking at | A valuable, item or loose object vanishes; an enemy is sent away. |
| Revive / Kill | Pick a player to revive or kill. Kill works once every 30 seconds. |
| Revive everyone | Every dead player comes back. |

## Levels & saves (HOST)

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Skip ahead | From a level to the shop, from the shop to the truck, from the truck to the next level. |
| Go to the shop / Go to level | Load the shop or any level right away. |
| Level number | Set which level of the run this is. |
| Next level is | Choose the level after the truck. |
| Leave out a level | Take levels out of the random rotation, or put them back. |
| Keep the save when everyone dies | The save stays instead of being deleted, as it was at the start of that level. |
| Back up every save | Each time the game saves, a copy is kept (the last 20 per save). You can also back up the current save by hand. |
| Restore a backup | From the main menu, put any backup back as the save, then load it as usual. |
| Restart this level | Load the current level again from scratch. |

## Extras

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Developer console | Unlocks the game's own developer console and its commands. |
| Unlock all cosmetics | Every cosmetic, saved in your own cosmetics save (your old save is backed up first). |

## Menu

A search box over every tab, profiles (save which cheats are on under a name and switch in one click), export and import of all settings through the clipboard, hotkeys for any switch or button, your own menu key, menu size and accent color, a list of active cheats on screen, short notifications, one button to switch everything off, and one to unload the menu from the game. Settings and hotkeys are saved in `%APPDATA%\STCheat`.
